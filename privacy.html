Privacy policy

This application is a personal-use OAuth client for rclone, used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the files in the owner's Google Drive that the application itself creates.
  • It does not collect, store, share or sell personal data of any person.
  • Files are transferred only between Google Drive and computers the owner controls. Nothing is sent to any other party.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, on this page or in the application.

Questions can be raised through the issue tracker of the repository that hosts this page.
